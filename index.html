Frequently asked questions

What you should know before running cs2-visual-assist on your setup.

Is cs2-visual-assist free to download?+
Yes. cs2-visual-assist is free and open source. The full external overlay — ESP boxes, skeleton chams, visibility check and team-color radar — is included in the public release available on the download page.
Which Windows versions are supported?+
The overlay targets Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). It runs as a standard external process with a DirectX overlay renderer, so no special drivers or kernel components need to be installed.
Does it claim to bypass VAC or anti-cheat?+
No. cs2-visual-assist makes no VAC bypass claims and does not include anti-cheat circumvention code. It is an external visual overlay project provided for research and educational use — using it in online matchmaking is at your own risk.
How do I update to the newest version?+
Download the latest build from https://gitscore.cc and replace your existing files. Your configuration is stored separately, so your ESP colors and radar settings carry over between versions.
